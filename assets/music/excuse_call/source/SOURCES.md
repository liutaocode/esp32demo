# Recorded sound credits

- **Incoming call - Marimba ringtone sound**, by **pogmothoin**, from [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/pogmothoin/sounds/351284/). Licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Creator credit: [kickblue22.com](https://www.kickblue22.com). The creator describes this as their own composition played on a Casio keyboard. Local source: `marimba.mp3`, public high-quality preview.
- **Phone vibration**, by **Breviceps**, from [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/515295/). Dedicated to the public domain under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Local source: `vibration.mp3`, public high-quality preview.

Changes: mono conversion, 16 kHz resampling, high-pass filtering, silence trimming, level normalization, pitch variations and mixing into five looping ringtones. The vibration sound is a recording of a smartphone, played through the speaker. No motor is controlled.

Retain these credits with distributed firmware and its public description. None of these recordings is claimed as our original recording or a proprietary phone-brand ringtone.
