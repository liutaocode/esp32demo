# Word Sprite



**Listen, learn, grow.** Turn a short English practice session into a little
adventure with a pixel companion. Pick Animal Forest, Food Town or School Planet,
listen to English, choose a Chinese meaning and light up the word collection.

The cover is a promotional illustration. The image below is a fresh runtime
capture of the installed firmware, acquired using the official serial protocol.



## How to play

1. Choose a world with Up and Down, then press OK. A normal round has six words.
2. Listen to the English word, select its Chinese meaning and press OK.
3. Hear Mandarin encouragement and an explanation, then repeat the English word.
   Hold Up on the feedback page to hear the bilingual explanation again.
4. Missed words stay in review. Hold Down at home or press Down on the result
   page to practise up to six missed words.
5. Finish a round, see your collection and sprite grow, photograph the growth
   card to share with family and take a break.

## What makes it fun

- **Grow together:** light up different words to grow an egg into a sprout,
  a winged sprite and a crowned sprite.
- **Understand the explanation:** every word has a spoken Chinese meaning.
  With instructions and encouragement, there are 41 Mandarin clips.
- **Find missed words again:** see and hear the right answer, then revisit it
  in review. There is no endless punishment round.
- **A small session, a small reward:** three worlds contain 36 basic words.
  Everything works offline without an account.

## Controls and tips

| Page | Up / Down | OK | Hold |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Choose world | Start | Down: review; Up: instructions |
| Question | Select meaning | Answer | Up: repeat English |
| Feedback | No action | Continue after reading | Up: bilingual explanation |
| Result | Up: another round; Down: review | Home | Up: instructions |

Hold OK to save and return home. Completing a round saves the collection and
missed words; wait for the saved indicator before switching off. Sudden power
loss during a round may lose that round's changes. The sprite changes at 6, 18
and 30 distinct lit words. Missing a previously lit word sends it back to review
and can lower the displayed growth stage. The collection records practice; it
does not certify lasting mastery. Speaking along is not pronunciation scoring.

The interface is in Simplified Chinese, for beginning learners who can read the
Chinese choices. Every round offers a natural place to stop.
